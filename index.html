About

I'm Ch Muhammad Dawood, a final-year Computer Science student at FAST NUCES focused on AI/ML engineering and full-stack product development. I work across the stack — from fine-tuning transformer models on low-resource languages to shipping production MERN and Android applications.

Most recently I built KhayaalNama, a journaling app that predicts mental-health signals from Roman-Urdu text using a fine-tuned XLM RoBERTa model, and Suraagh, an AI-driven lost-and-found platform with image and text matching.

Off-screen I read and write Urdu poetry, lead the FAST Literary Society, and pick up new languages for fun.
